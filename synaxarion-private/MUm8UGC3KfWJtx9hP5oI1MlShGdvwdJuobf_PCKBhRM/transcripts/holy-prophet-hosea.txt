In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, amen.
The reading from the Sinaxanion for October 17th, on which we commemorate the Holy Prophet
Hosea, the Holy Prophet Hosea, the Son of Berea, was of the tribe of Izekar, and lived
in the reigns of Jeroboam, son of Joash, and of the kings of the breakaway kingdom of Israel,
or Samaria, that came after him, circa the year 734 BC.
Like the other prophets, Hosea was the interpreter of the messages of God to his people, not
in word alone, but indeed also.
In his life, Hosea showed forth the mysterious purposes of God, for the Lord commanded
him to marry a harlot who would be unfaithful to him, just as the people of Israel were unfaithful
to their covenant with God by giving themselves over to idolatry.
In broken-hearted tones, Hosea gives voice in his prophecy to the disappointed love of
the Lord, and to his threats of dire punishments, awaiting the adulterous people, namely foreign invasion
and exile.
But God chasens only to save his love for the people he has chosen to himself is too great
to exercise the fierceness of his anger.
God will again lead his people into the wilderness, exile, and as if to an airing wife, a lure
her and speak tenderly to her so that in repentance and tears she may return forever to her divine
bridegroom and eternally rejoice in his peace, his tenderness, and his love.
In those days, God will say to the church, Thou art my people, and she will reply, Thou
art my God.
This reconciliation between God and his people will only truly come to pass, Hosea affirms,
when Christ comes to release mankind from the power of death.
When we will be able to cry out in the light of Easter day, death is swallowed up in victory.
O death, where is thy sting, O grave, where is thy victory?
When he had fulfilled the mission that God entrusted to him, Hosea fell asleep in peace and
was buried in the land of his fathers.
In bodily form he resembled Joachim, the father of the mother of God, and even more closely
Joseph, her husband, according to the law.
Through the prayers of our holy fathers, O Lord, Jesus Christ our God, have mercy on us
and save us.
