The reading from the Sinaxadion for October 19th, on which we commemorate the Holy Prophet
Joel.
On the 19th of the month, we celebrate the memory of the Holy Prophet Joel.
The Holy Prophet Joel, the Son of Bethel, belonged to the tribe of Rubin.
He prophesied in the kingdom of Judah, above all in Jerusalem, at a date that cannot be
determined exactly.
At the beginning of Joel's prophecy, God tells his people that the plague of locusts,
which is devastating the land, is a sign of the dreadful chastisements that will proceed
and the day of the Lord, the day when the Lord will come to judge the earth.
God's threats are to warn the people of the need for true repentance.
Return to me with all your heart and with fasting and with weeping and with mourning and
rend your hearts and not your garments.
Return to the Lord your God for he is gracious and merciful, slow to anger and of great kindness
and repents him of the evil.
Beyond these threats, the Prophet allows us to glimpse the prospect of the future restoration
of divine favor in the outpouring of the Holy Spirit upon all.
And it will come to pass afterward that I will pour out my spirit on all flesh.
Your sons and your daughters shall prophesy.
Your old men shall dream dreams.
Your young men shall see visions.
This is the blessed age which has its origin at Pentecost and that will come to fulfillment
in the day of the second coming of Christ.
Through the prayers of our holy fathers, O Lord, Jesus Christ our God, have mercy on us
and save us.
