In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, amen.
The reading from the Sinaxadion for October 14, on which we commemorate our holy mother
Pescheva the Younger.
Saint Pescheva, born in Thrace at the beginning of the 11th century, came of a rich aristocratic
family.
As a child, she advanced to such a height in the holy evangelic virtues that when she was
no more than ten years old, unknown to her parents, she exchanged her rich attire for
rags like those of the beggars who ask alms of the people coming out of church.
She left her family and all she had and made her way to Constantinople to venerate the
many precious relics that were to be found there.
She went on to Heraclia in Pontus to evade her parents' search and stayed there for
five years, living in solitude, fasting, vigils, tears and unceasing prayer near a little
church dedicated to the mother of God.
Like every Christian she desired with all her heart to worship at the places where the
Savior walked, and so left her retreat to go to Jerusalem.
She settled in a monastery in the Jordan desert where the nuns lived in a very strict askeases
that answered to her desire to advance in virtue so as to attain the wondrous heights
of holy humility and of divine love.
When she was 25 years old, she left for Japa or Jafa and took ship for her home country
of Thrace.
Sometime later she went once more to venerate the relics of saints at Constantinople and
settled in a village called Calicratia near a church dedicated to the Holy Apostles.
Two years later she gave up her soul in peace into the hands of the Holy Angels who conducted
her to the heavenly mansions.
Her holy relics were discovered after a vision many years later.
They worked innumerable miracles and were taken to Belgrade.
When that city fell to the Turks they were returned to Constantinople and in the year 1641
she was removed to Yasi in Moldavia.
Saint Pulaskiva is now venerated as the patron saint of Romania.
Through the prayers of our holy fathers, O Lord Jesus Christ our God, have mercy on us
and save us.
