In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, Amen.
The reading from the Sinaxadion, for October 11, on which we commemorate.
The Holy Apostle, Philip, the Deacon.
On the 11th of the month we celebrate the memory of the Holy Apostle, Philip, one of the seven
deacons.
The Saint Philip was born at Cessaria in Palestine.
He was married and had four daughters who consecrated their virginity to God and were given the
gift of prophecy.
The Apostles ordained him Deacon at the same time as Stephen, to assist in the service
of tables and in charitable works.
On the Apostolic College, dispersed to proclaim the gospel, Philip spread the glad tidings
of salvation throughout Samaria.
He baptized Simon, the magician, who feigned conversion in the hope of making money by the
gifts of the Holy Spirit.
Then the Lord sent him along the Gaza Road to the minister of the Ethiopian Queen Candace,
as he traveled south from Jerusalem in his chariot, reading the prophecy of Isaiah.
Philip gave him the key to the mysterious words of the prophet about the Savior, baptized
him and was caught away by the Spirit to continue his preaching to Azotus.
From there he evangelized all the cities through which he passed till he came to Cessaria.
After that, he made his way to the province of Tralia in Asia Minor.
There he led a great many pagans to the knowledge of God, built a church and fell asleep in peace.
Through the prayers of our Holy Father's, O Lord Jesus Christ our God, have mercy on us
and save us.
