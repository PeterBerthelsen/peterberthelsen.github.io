In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, Amen.
The reading from the Sinaxadion for October 22, on which we commemorate our venerable
Father Lot, the memory of our venerable Father Lot, who died in peace, Saint Lot lived
as an ascetic in Egypt, at the same time as Saint Rufus.
They tell of a brother who went to see Abba Lot, so distressed about a sin he had committed
that he could not sit down, but kept going in and out of the cell.
What is the matter, brother?
Abba Lot asked him.
I have committed a great sin, he replied, that I cannot admit to the fathers.
Confess it to me, and I will bear it, the old man said to him.
I have fallen into fornication, the brother told him, and in order to do so, I sacrificed
to the idols.
The elder reassured and encouraged him, have confidence, he said.
No sin is beyond the mercy of God, repentance is possible, go and sit in the cave, eat only
one day in two, and we will both take an equal share of the burden of your offense.
After three weeks, Abba Lot received assurance from God that he had accepted the brother's
repentance, and, for the rest of his days, that brother remained in obedience to him.
One day, Abba Lot went to ask a word of Abba Joseph.
To the question, what shall I do to be saved?
The old man got up, and stretched out his hands towards heaven.
His fingers became as ten flames of fire, and he said to him,
You cannot become a monk, unless you become altogether like a fire that consumes itself.
To the prayers of our holy fathers, O Lord, Jesus Christ our God, have mercy on us, and save us.
