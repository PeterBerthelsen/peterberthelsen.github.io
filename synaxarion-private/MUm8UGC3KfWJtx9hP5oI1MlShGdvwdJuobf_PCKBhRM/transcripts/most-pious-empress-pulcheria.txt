The memory of the most pious empress, Pulcheria, who died in peace.
And Pulcheria was the sister of Emperor Thodosios II in the years 408 to 450.

She saw to it that the young Thodosius and his two other sisters, Arcadia and Maria, were
brought up in godliness and modesty.
She encouraged her sisters to remain in lifelong virginity as she did herself.
On the death of Thodosios, she married the Emperor Marcian in the years 450 through 457.
A good man, a no longer young, with whom she lived in chastity, with the same love of godliness
and the same dedication to the church.
Throughout her life, she expended the most part of her wealth in charitable works and was
a zealous builder of churches, hospitals, orphanages and monasteries.
Her zeal in preserving the purity of the Orthodox faith was just as ardent.

monophysites, and during its sessions she took her place with Marcian in the midst of the
